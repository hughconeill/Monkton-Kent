# Parish Council Chairmen 1894 – 2026

One of the Parish Council special booklets listed the chairmen who have served
the village of Monkton since 1894 – and where they lived.

| Years | Chairman | Home |
|---|---|---|
| 1894 – 1899 | John Lamb | “The Post Office” |
| 1900 – 1909 | Henry Thomas Willett | “Parsonage Farm” |
| 1910 – 1912 | Frederick Fuller | “Fernlea” |
| 1913 | Albert Henry Burton | “The Forge House” |
| 1914 – 1945 | Henry Thomas Willett | “Parsonage Farm” |
| 1946 – 1948 | Herbert Allnutt Smith | “Monkton Court” |
| 1949 – 1954 | William J Cole | “Rosslyn” |
| 1955 – 1968 | Frederick A Fuller | “Elmbank” |
| 1969 – 1975 | Bernard J Cole | “Wilbernam” |
| 1976 | Michael Jeffrey Smith | “Monkton Court” |
| 1977 | Bernard J Cole | “Wilbernam” |
| 1978 | Julie Cook (Mrs) | “Seamark Close” |
| 1979 – 1984 | Bernard J Cole | “Wilbernam” |
| 1985 – 1986 | John W Knight | “Rose Cottage” |
| 1987 – 1991 | Anthony Turner | “Seamark Close” |
| 1992 – 1999 | Ken Gregory | “Golden Point Farm” |
| 1999 – | Robin Tapsell | “Waggon Cottage” |

*Compiled by Stuart Horsburgh. Many of these homes appear on the
