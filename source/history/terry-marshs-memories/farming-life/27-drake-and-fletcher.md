# 27. Drake and Fletcher

You have to have lived in Monkton for nearly 30 years to remember the agricultural engineering works, Drake and Fletcher, that were situated opposite the pub. Drake and Fletcher were Vauxhall car dealers with an establishment in the Maidstone area. In the early 1950's they saw that in the Thanet area there was an opportunity to set up an agricultural engineering workshop. They had a franchise for selling and repairing David Brown tractors and sold quite a range of farm machinery. They also had a shop that, not only sold spare parts for the machines, but sold everything from nuts and bolts, spanners, gardening equipment and outdoor clothing. Over the next 20 or so years, the works grew, with a good reputation, employing some very good fitters.

I don't know exactly what happened in the first half of the 1970's, but they lost several of their best fitters. My understanding was that they were charging rather high prices for their repair work and the fitters were being paid almost agricultural wages. Some of the larger farms didn't have their own fitters and had most of their repair work done at Drake and Fletchers. Some of these farms offered the fitters more money to work for them on their farms. Brian Croucher went to work for Baxters at Sarre and Peter Lawerence went to Clifftons at Abbey Farm in Minster (two of them that I knew).

It was during this period of the early 70's that the blacksmiths forge, that was situated between the Post Office and the New Inn was demolished. The blacksmith, Fred Harrison, and all his equipment was relocated to the rear of the Drake and Fletcher works.

The business struggled for a few years after this and came under new management at the end of the 70's. David Pitcher became manager and David Egglesdon took over the sales side of the business. They won the franchise to sell John Deere tractors and combines in this part of Kent whilst also selling well known makes of farm machinery.

Unfortunately, in 1998 they lost the franchise for selling John Deere's and were forced to close in October of that year.

On Sheriffs Court Farm we then had two tractors, a rough terrain loader and a lawn mower that were John Deere's and our nearest suppliers/fitters became Bell Engineering which were based at Appledore.

Fortunately, Robert Norrington, who had spent all his working life at Drake and Fletcher, set himself up as 'Thanet Farm Repairs'. Robert is a very good fitter and we used him for all of our repair work right up until I retired in 2018. In all that time we never had to call Robert up to say that this piece of equipment was still not working properly.

*Terry Marsh*
