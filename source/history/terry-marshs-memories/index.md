# Terry Marsh's Memories

**Two series of articles by Terry Marsh, from the Monkton News**

Terry Marsh grew up in Monkton and spent his working life on the farms around
it. Between 2022 and 2026 he wrote two long-running columns for the Monkton
News parish magazine: first Monkton Memories, his recollections of growing up
in the village, and then A Farming Life, his working life on local farms from
1976 until he retired in 2018. Both series are reproduced here in full.

```{admonition} Complete – for now!
:class: tip

With the November 2026 issue of the Monkton News, every one of Terry's
articles is now on this website: 22 instalments of Monkton Memories and 27
of A Farming Life. Terry has promised to make a reappearance some day – we
hope he does!
```
