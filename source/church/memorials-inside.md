# Memorials Inside the Church

Fifteen memorial tablets, floor stones and brasses survive inside St Mary
Magdalene, as well as the brass of John Spicer, a medieval vicar, described at the end of this page.
Numbering below follows the church's own memorial numbering
(1–15), used consistently across this page and the 
[Churchyard](churchyard/index.md) records, since several families have memorials
in both places.

## Full transcriptions

Inscriptions are transcribed as closely as possible to the original wording and line breaks (marked `/`); a `?` marks a word that is uncertain or illegible. Genealogical notes drawn from the parish baptism, marriage, and burial registers are added in *italic brackets* after each entry.

## Brass of John Spicer

*\[ on the floor of the nave, just before the chancel step \]*

The brass shows John Spicer, [Vicar of Monkton](clergy.md) from 1427 to 1451, in his
vestments. A framed rubbing of the brass hangs on the north wall of the nave by the church
door, with this caption:

*"This Brass rubbing is reproduced from the Brass in the nave of St Mary Magdalene Church,
Monkton and is John Spicer, Vicar of Monkton from 1427 to 1451, (died 1460), in his vestments.*

*It is printed in this limited numbered edition of 200 only in AD 1988 to commemorate the
commencement of the restoration work on the Church."*
