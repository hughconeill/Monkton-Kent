# Named Memorial Stones in Numerical Order

A condensed index of the churchyard by plot number: names and year of
death only. For full inscriptions see the
[survey by location](survey-by-location.md); for genealogical detail see
the [alphabetical name index](names-alphabetical.md) and the
[register appendix](register-appendix.md).

```{admonition} About unnumbered rows
:class: note

A few rows have no stone number — these are extra graves the survey found
without a numbered marker. For these, the Stone No. column instead gives
their location relative to a numbered stone (e.g. "b/n #94 & seat" —
between stone #94 and the seat). They're kept in walking order alongside
the numbered entries.
```
