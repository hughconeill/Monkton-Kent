# Downloads

The material on this website is also available as three printable volumes,
which you are welcome to download, print and share. Each is an A4 PDF with a
contents page, and includes the photographs that appear alongside the text
on the website (the full photo collection is in the [Gallery](gallery.md)).

The website is a living record that is refreshed and expanded as more
information comes in, so the volumes are reissued from time to time — check
the version shown below (and in the footer of every page of the PDF) against
this page for the latest copy.

### The Church of St Mary Magdalene

*Monkton, Thanet – with the churchyard survey*

The church guide, its bells and clergy since 1291, Richard Culmer, the war memorial with a short biography of every man named on it, and the memorials inside the church — followed by Stuart Horsburgh's full churchyard survey as a reference section (graves by location, an alphabetical index of names, the numbered stones and the burial register appendix).

### Monkton: A Folk History

*by Stuart Horsburgh*

Stuart Horsburgh's research: the house-by-house Centenary Mile Walk of 1926 down Monkton Street, special stories, Bernard Cole's interviews with villagers, and the Parish Council chairmen since 1894.

[Download the PDF](_static/downloads/monkton-folk-history-stuart-horsburgh.pdf) – 105 pages, 2.5 MB

### Monkton Memories & A Farming Life

*by Terry Marsh*

Terry Marsh's two columns from the Monkton News: Monkton Memories, his recollections of growing up in the village, and A Farming Life, his working life on local farms from 1976 onwards.

[Download the PDF](_static/downloads/monkton-memories-and-a-farming-life-terry-marsh.pdf) – 65 pages, 0.9 MB

## Before you print

```{note}
The information in these volumes is accurate to the best of our knowledge,
but it draws on old records, gravestones and memories that are sometimes
incomplete or disagree with one another. If you spot an error, or can add to
anything, please get in touch — contact details are on the
[About](about.md) page.

Text and photographs remain the copyright of their authors and contributors,
who have shared them for the benefit of the village. The volumes are free to
download, print and share for personal, educational and non-commercial use;
please ask before reproducing material elsewhere. Contributors are thanked on
the [Credits](credits.md) page and at the back of each volume.
```

The churchyard survey volume is long — the reference section at the back
runs to many pages of tables — so you may prefer to print only the pages
you need.
