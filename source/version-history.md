# Version History

This site is updated as new material is gathered and errors are corrected. The main
releases are listed below, newest first.

| Version | Date | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | 5 November 2026 | Minor update: corrections to the church and churchyard pages, many more photographs in the [Gallery](gallery.md), a [mailing list](mailing-list.md) so you can hear about future updates, and this version history page. |
| 1.0 | November 2026 | First public version, launched in the November 2026 issue of the Monkton News. It included Stuart Horsburgh's history of the church and survey of the churchyard, his 1926 Centenary trail, Monkton's history from web sources, Terry Marsh's Monkton News columns, Monkton's listed buildings, other Monktons around the world, and War Graves Commission references for the war graves in the churchyard. |
