# Version History

This site is updated as new material is gathered and errors are corrected. The main
releases are listed below, newest first.

| Version | Date | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | December 2026 | Terry Marsh's two Monkton News series are now grouped together as [Terry Marsh's Memories](history/terry-marshs-memories/index.md), and the last two instalments of [A Farming Life](history/terry-marshs-memories/farming-life/index.md) – [Farming Fires](history/terry-marshs-memories/farming-life/26-farming-fires.md) (October 2026) and [Drake and Fletcher](history/terry-marshs-memories/farming-life/27-drake-and-fletcher.md) (November 2026) – complete his articles, for now we hope! Also new: tips on using the website on the home page. The printable volumes have been refreshed to match. |
| **2.0** | December 2026 | Released in the December 2026 issue of the Monkton News. New [Stuart's Research](history/stuarts-research/index.md) section, replacing the single Monkton 1926 page: Stuart Horsburgh's folk history of Monkton, with the [Centenary Mile Walk of 1926](history/stuarts-research/centenary-walk/index.md) now house by house in four zones (census records from 1841 to 1994), [special stories](history/stuarts-research/special-stories.md), [Bernard Cole's interviews](history/stuarts-research/bernard-cole-interviews.md) with villagers from 1989 and 1991, and the [Parish Council chairmen](history/stuarts-research/parish-council-chairmen.md) since 1894. New photographs in the [Gallery](gallery.md), more contributors thanked on the [Credits](credits.md) page, and a new [Downloads](downloads.md) page offering the website as three printable PDF volumes. |
| 1.1 | 5 November 2026 | Minor update: corrections to the church and churchyard pages, many more photographs in the [Gallery](gallery.md), a [mailing list](mailing-list.md) so you can hear about future updates, and this version history page. |
| **1.0** | November 2026 | First public version, launched in the November 2026 issue of the Monkton News. It included Stuart Horsburgh's history of the church and survey of the churchyard, his 1926 Centenary trail, Monkton's history from web sources, Terry Marsh's Monkton News columns, Monkton's listed buildings, other Monktons around the world, and War Graves Commission references for the war graves in the churchyard. |
